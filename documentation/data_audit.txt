### Observations

- BikeWithRider is the most frequently annotated class, accounting for 45.8% of all annotations.
- Helmet is the least frequently annotated class, accounting for 20.2% of all annotations.
- The class distribution is uneven, so we should monitor per-class precision and recall during model evaluation.
- These percentages represent the distribution of annotated objects, not the percentage of images containing each class.
- Class imbalance alone does not establish that resampling or other balancing techniques are necessary.
